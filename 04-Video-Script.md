# Promo Video Script
## "A Day With Your Chief of Staff"

**Runtime:** ~50–60 seconds
**Subject:** Shawn Getty, Getty Group, Calgary
**Production:** phone camera, natural light, real locations. Agent voice via ElevenLabs.
**Tone:** calm, unhurried, cinematic. No music stings, no feature callouts, no text overlays until the end.

---

### SHOT 1 — Bedroom, pre-dawn
**Visual:** Dark. Thin dawn light through blinds. Close on a phone face-up on the nightstand. It lights up — not an alarm. Soft music rising from it.

**AGENT (V.O.):**
> Morning, Shawn. Slept alright? Before anything else — here's your track.

**Note:** the phone speaking, not buzzing, is the whole hook. Hold this shot longer than feels comfortable.

---

### SHOT 2 — Bedroom / window
**Visual:** He's up. Curtains open. City waking below.

**AGENT (V.O.):**
> You said you're training at seven. You've got forty minutes. Nothing urgent came in overnight.

**Note:** this is the "it doesn't jump straight into work" beat. It matters more than it looks.

---

### SHOT 3 — Kitchen
**Visual:** Coffee. Morning light. He's leaning on the counter, not looking at the phone.

**SHAWN:**
> What's happening today?

**AGENT (V.O.):**
> Ottawa held rates yesterday — expect buyers to move. Calgary inventory's up four percent this week. And three agent prospects replied overnight. One's worth your time.

---

### SHOT 4 — Car, driving ★ *(centrepiece)*
**Visual:** Interior. Hands on the wheel. Radio low. City going past. The phone rings through the car speakers — and it's the agent calling *him*.

**AGENT (V.O.):**
> Shawn, sorry to interrupt. That Bridgeland listing — a lead came through twenty minutes ago. I've already spoken to her. She's pre-approved with a local bank, and she's free Thursday afternoon.

**SHAWN:**
> Is she serious?

**AGENT (V.O.):**
> She's viewed three properties this month and made one offer that fell through. I'd say yes.

**SHAWN:**
> Book her in.

**AGENT (V.O.):**
> Done. I'll send you her file before you get there.

**Visual:** He glances at the phone. Small smile. Eyes back on the road.

**Note:** hands never leave the wheel. That's the beat — a deal moved forward and he did nothing. This shot is the sale. Give it the most screen time.

---

### SHOT 5 — Office entrance
**Visual:** He pushes open the office door, coffee in hand. Text appears on screen, one line at a time, quietly:

> While he drove —
> lead qualified
> viewing booked
> follow-ups sent
> blog drafted

---

### SHOT 6 — Black
**Visual:** Cut to black. Beat of silence.

**AGENT (V.O.), quieter:**
> You've got a business. Now you've got a chief of staff.

**Visual:** Logo. One line: *$125/month.* URL.

---

## Production Notes

- **Real footage, synthetic voice.** Film Shawn for real; generate the agent's voice with ElevenLabs. The one thing that *should* sound synthetic is the only thing that is.
- **The agent never sounds robotic or servile.** It sounds like a sharp colleague who's already done the work. "I'd say yes" is the most important line in the script — it has an opinion.
- **No UI on screen.** Never show an app interface. The moment you show a dashboard it becomes software again.
- **Shoot order:** car scene first while light is good and Shawn is fresh. It's the shot that matters.
- **Alternate cut:** a 15-second version using only Shot 4 for Instagram and paid social.
