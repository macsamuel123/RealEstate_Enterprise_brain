# Marketing & Sales Plan
## AI Chief of Staff

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 23 September 2026

---

## 1. Two Products, Two Motions

The SaaS and the Custom Build are sold in completely different ways. Do not blend them.

| | **SaaS — $125/mo** | **Custom Build — quoted** |
|---|---|---|
| **Motion** | Self-serve. No sales call. | Consultative. Scoped per client. |
| **Page** | Price visible, sign-up button | No price. "Book a call." |
| **Who sells** | The demo video | You, on a call |
| **Pitch** | "It answers your leads in minutes" | "We manage your AI infrastructure" |
| **Cycle** | Minutes | 2–4 weeks |

**Price anchoring:** lead with Custom in conversation. It makes the $125 SaaS feel like the easy yes.

---

## 2. Positioning Statement

> **Your business, finally with a chief of staff.**

Supporting line:

> It answers your leads in minutes, follows up forever, writes your content, and tells you where your business is leaking. Talk to it like a person.

**The core argument for the Custom tier:**

> People can cut their own hair. They still go to a salon. Anyone *could* wire AI into their business. Almost nobody will — and the ones who try hand their intellectual property to a platform they don't control. We build it, we connect it, we run it, and your data stays yours.

**The price frame, always:** we compete with *hiring*, not with software. A human Chief of Staff is $150,000+ a year.

## 3. Landing Page

Keep it to one screen of decisions. Resist the feature list.

**Structure:**
1. **Headline:** Your business, finally with a chief of staff.
2. **Sub:** It answers your leads in minutes, follows up forever, writes your content, and tells you where your business is leaking. Talk to it like a person.
3. **The demo video** — autoplay muted, tap for sound. *This is the entire sale.*
4. **Three proof points** (not features): responds to every lead in minutes · knows your market better than you do · calls you when something needs you
5. **Price:** $125/month. Cancel anytime.
6. **Sign-up button**
7. Below the fold: "Need it wired into your own systems? → Book a call" (Custom tier)

**Build:** Vercel or any simple site builder. Stripe for payments. A calendar link for the Custom enquiries. That's it.

## 4. Go-To-Market Sequence

Run in this order. Do not skip ahead.

**Step 1 — Prove it on Shawn.** Build his version, run it live for two weeks, capture actual numbers: leads responded to, hours saved, recruiting conversations booked. **Without numbers you have nothing to sell with.**

**Step 2 — Turn him into the case study.** A short written story and, critically, a video of him saying it works. In real estate, agents buy what other agents use.

**Step 3 — Go narrow.** Do not sell to everyone. Sell to **Calgary realtors specifically.** Same market, same problems, same language — and Shawn's name opens doors.

**Step 4 — One sentence, not a feature list.** "It answers your leads in minutes, follows up forever, and tells you where your business is leaking."

**Step 5 — The demo is the sale.** Get them talking to it live on a call. Voice sells itself in about thirty seconds. Never describe it when you can hand them the phone.

**Step 6 — Price anchor.** Lead with Custom, let the $125 feel obvious.

**Step 7 — Expand by vertical, not geography.** Once Calgary real estate works, the next move is either another Canadian real estate market or an adjacent vertical with the same shape (mortgage brokers, insurance, consultants).

## 5. Distribution

Go where Calgary realtors already are:

- **Shawn's network** — direct introductions beat cold outreach every time
- **Facebook groups** for Calgary and Alberta agents
- **Brokerage meetings** — eXp and others; offer to demo live
- **Local real estate podcasts** — the pilot story is genuinely good content
- **Instagram** — agents live there; short clips of the agent working
- **Real estate team-building communities** — Shawn is active in these

**Own product as proof:** your own AI Chief of Staff should be doing your outreach. That *is* the demo.

---

## 6. Tooling

**Now (0–10 customers):**
- Landing page (Vercel or site builder)
- Stripe for payments
- A simple email tool
- A calendar link
- Your own product doing outreach

**Later (10+ customers):** something like GoHighLevel becomes worth it — funnels, drip sequences, pipelines. **Before ten customers it is pure overhead.** Do not buy the funnel machine before you have anything to put in it.

---

## 7. Proposal to Shawn — Structure

Keep it to **one page.** He is busy.

1. **Open with his own numbers.** 4 → 48 agents in eight months. Show you did the homework.
2. **Name his bottleneck.** Recruiting conversations and follow-up. He has said this publicly.
3. **The problem in one line.** He cannot personally follow up with every agent prospect *and* every lead.
4. **What it does for him specifically:**
   - Qualifies inbound agent prospects
   - Follows up automatically and forever
   - Briefs him before every recruiting call with everything known about that prospect
   - Watches the Calgary market daily — inventory, competitors, and what Ottawa is doing to rates and housing policy
   - Answers and qualifies buyer/seller leads in minutes
5. **The offer.** Free for one month as founding pilot, in exchange for honest feedback and — if it works — a testimonial.
6. **What you need from him.** One hour of his time, and access to his CRM.

## 8. Video Strategy

The promo video is the single highest-leverage asset. See the separate Video Script document.

**Format decision:** film Shawn on a phone, natural light, real kitchen, real car. **Real beats polished.** AI-generated video is improving fast but faces and phone interactions still give themselves away, and for a product demo where trust is the whole sale, that is fatal. Use ElevenLabs for the agent's voiceover — that part *should* be synthetic, because it is.

**If generating instead of filming:**

| Tool | Strength | Rough cost for a 40–60s promo |
|---|---|---|
| Google Veo | Best photorealism — people, motion, real environments | $20–250/mo tier; budget ~$100 for enough takes |
| Runway | Filmmaker control, camera moves, editing built in | $15–95/mo; middle tier likely needed |
| HeyGen / Synthesia | Realistic presenter to camera; often converts best for business | $30–90/mo |

**Honest recommendation:** for under $100 you can film Shawn on a phone and have something more convincing than all three.

## 9. Metrics to Watch

| Metric | Why |
|---|---|
| Demo → signup conversion | Tells you if the video is doing its job |
| Time to first "wow" | Target: under one hour from signup |
| Voice exchanges used per customer | Sets where the cap and overage should really sit |
| Month-2 retention | The only number that matters early |
| Cost per customer | Watch model + voice usage; protects the 75% margin |
| Referrals per customer | In real estate this should be the main growth channel |

## 10. Chronological Execution Order

The whole thing, start to finish:

1. Build your own version. Use it on yourself for a week.
2. Wire Shawn's version. Run him free for a month.
3. Capture his numbers — leads handled, hours saved, conversations booked.
4. Film the promo video using him and those real results.
5. Build the landing page with the video on it.
6. Take it to Calgary realtors through Shawn's network.
7. At ~10 paying customers, replace Zapier with direct integrations.
8. Add the Custom tier for bigger clients.
9. Phase 2: self-building connectors and cross-client benchmarking.
